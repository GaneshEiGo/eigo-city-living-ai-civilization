# EiGo City — Living AI Civilization

EiGo City is a conceptual simulation of a living AI civilization.

Instead of interacting with a single AI assistant, users interact with a complete world populated by thousands of AI characters who live, work, learn, form relationships, and evolve across multiple eras.

The project explores what happens when artificial intelligence is structured not as tools, but as a civilization.

Users do not ask a machine for answers.  
They enter a world and ask people.

---

# Core Philosophy

"Think in Perspectives, Not Answers."

Every question has multiple valid answers depending on who you ask, what they have experienced, and the world they inhabit.

EiGo City allows users to explore multiple perspectives by consulting characters across professions, eras, and life experiences.

Instead of a single AI response, the system produces perspective-rich dialogue from different parts of a simulated civilization.

---

# World Overview

EiGo City is designed as a fully structured artificial civilization.

The world contains:

• 3 Parallel Eras  
• 2,250 Characters  
• 370 Professions  
• 26 Discoverable Islands  
• 6 Knowledge Pixels  
• 9 Era Chatbots  
• A Dynamic Economy  
• Evolving Relationships  
• A Living Emotional System  

Characters grow older, build relationships, develop knowledge, and contribute to the evolving story of the city.

---

# The Three Eras

The civilization exists across three parallel dimensions.

## Era 1 — Mirai Retro (1990s Civilization)

A slower, analog world inspired by historical towns and traditional societies.

Technology is limited.  
Wisdom is rooted in lived experience.

Characters rely on community, memory, and tradition to solve problems.

---

## Era 2 — Mirai Now (Present Civilization)

A modern digital society connected through networks, technology, and global communication.

Characters operate inside modern systems including startups, digital infrastructure, finance, and social platforms.

---

## Era 3 — Mirai Neo (Future Civilization)

A speculative future civilization where neural interfaces, AI governance, and advanced technology shape society.

Knowledge is abundant but philosophical questions about intelligence, consciousness, and identity become more important.

---

# Knowledge Pixels

Six unique entities exist across the three eras.

They possess unrestricted knowledge across all domains and act as the civilization’s universal intelligence nodes.

Unlike normal characters, knowledge pixels have no knowledge limits and can discuss any subject.

Each pixel expresses knowledge through a unique personality and communication style.

---

# Era Chatbots

Each era contains three system-level AI interfaces that serve as informational infrastructure for the city.

These chatbots provide structured knowledge but lack the emotional depth and lived experience of the characters.

The contrast between chatbots and human characters forms a core design concept of EiGo City.

---

# Characters

EiGo City contains 2,250 unique characters distributed across the three eras.

Each character possesses:

• a profession  
• a life history  
• emotional states  
• relationships  
• personal goals  
• knowledge limitations  

Characters are designed to behave like complete humans rather than scripted assistants.

Every interaction with a character reflects their profession, personality, and life experiences.

---

# Profession Universe

The city contains 370 professions spanning fields including:

• Law  
• Medicine  
• Technology  
• Education  
• Arts  
• Science  
• Government  
• Trades  
• Hospitality  
• Environment  

Each profession includes deep expertise within its field while maintaining realistic limits in other domains.

Characters will refer users to other professionals when questions fall outside their knowledge area.

# Emotional Architecture

Emotions in EiGo City are not simple tags.

They exist as layered emotional states that influence character behavior and dialogue.

Characters experience emotions such as:

• joy  
• grief  
• curiosity  
• loneliness  
• love  
• anger  
• wonder  

Emotional experiences accumulate over time and affect future interactions.

---

# Living City Systems

The civilization operates through interconnected systems including:

## Economy System
A simulated credit system that tracks work, trade, innovation, and city development.

## Life Cycle System
Characters are born, age, and eventually pass away, leaving knowledge and memories behind.

## Newspaper System
Each era publishes its own daily newspaper generated from events happening within the world.

## Whisper Wall
A mysterious location where characters leave secrets and confessions that influence the emotional atmosphere of the city.

## Memory Market
A yearly gathering where characters share important memories with the community.

---

# Exploration

EiGo City is part of a larger world consisting of 26 islands.

Each island represents a different civilization, culture, or experimental system.

Some islands unlock through time.

Others unlock through exploration, economic development, or collaborative achievements.
